I wanted to share a prototype I’ve been building - an Ecosystem Explorer
https://ecosystem-explorer.streamlit.app/

It’s a simple tool for exploring tradeoffs in urban land-use strategies, specifically how different allocations across green infrastructure, food forests, and development affect flood risk, cooling, food production, and cost.

The current version uses Minneapolis data, but the structure is meant to be flexible so we can plug in other cities (like San Antonio) as data becomes available.

If you have a few minutes, I’d really appreciate your reactions, especially:
	•	whether the outputs feel meaningful and understandable
	•	whether this seems useful for exploring scenarios (like the San Antonio work)
	•	what directions would make it more valuable (e.g., adding new cities, incorporating heat vulnerability indices, introducing cost-effectiveness metrics like $/acre-foot of runoff prevented)
